{% extends "base_parent.html" %} {% comment %} The family's landing screen — RFP §2.3. The July 28 reference used a deliberately short journey: choose a child, then choose the thing to see. That is restored here using only routes already available in this Django application. It keeps the home useful for a parent on a phone instead of making them scan a long mixed feed. **How this differs from the child profile, and why it has to.** The two screens are one journey and they share a vocabulary, so the risk is that the home becomes a worse copy of the profile. They are split by the question each answers: home — "what has happened lately?" greeting, announcements, recent moments, the last few observations, a way in to everything profile — "who is my child?" identity, About Me, ages 2–5, development, enrolment history So the home leads with a greeting and carries the announcements, which the profile does not have at all; and the child appears here as a compact card that opens the profile, not as the profile's full hero. Nothing here repeats About Me, the age pages, the assessments or the enrolment history. The mockup also draws a daily summary with temperature and mood, upcoming events, a teacher post feed with likes and comments, a payment card and an app-store banner. Those are Phase 2 and 3 (docs/design/INDEX.md); a card that renders empty on a family's home screen teaches them the system is broken, so none of them is faked here. {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Нүүр хуудас{% endblock %} {% block heading %}Нүүр хуудас{% endblock %} {% block content %} {% include "components/child_switcher.html" %}
